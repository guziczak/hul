Nature is the new luxury

We believe a healthy home begins with natural materials — timeless materials that gain character as the years go by. That is why, for more than 30 years, our workshop has designed and crafted bespoke kitchens made entirely from solid wood.

Before your kitchen design takes shape

Our work begins with selecting raw tree trunks from nearby forests, which we then cut and season ourselves. It is the only way to maintain complete control over what goes into your interior. This gives you not only a healthy microclimate at home, but also the technical assurance that your kitchen will remain stable for decades.

The Hul kitchen-making process

We start with the wood and a conversation

Stage 1

Getting to know each other

You can visit our studio at the DOMAR Interior Design Gallery, call us or email us. We talk about your home, your needs and your vision of the ideal kitchen. On that basis, we outline possible design directions and the scope of the project, making sure that our approach to creating interiors fully matches your expectations.

Stage 2

Designing and refining your vision

During a visit to your home, our architect takes precise measurements that become the basis for the design and initial visualisations. Through a thoughtful process, we refine the layout, materials and details. We take care of every technical aspect, from planning the services to choosing the right appliances. Our team guides you through every decision, ensuring the entire concept is cohesive and timeless.

Stage 3

Technical documentation

Once the design has been refined, we prepare detailed technical documentation. Our architects work directly with the cabinetmakers so that every element is carefully planned. Precise construction drawings are created to guide the subsequent stages of production.

Stage 4

Craftsmanship

The wood prepared earlier becomes your kitchen. We combine the precision of modern CNC machinery with skilled craftsmanship, choosing the right method for the character of each element. This allows us to achieve lasting strength and accuracy, whatever the style of the kitchen. At this stage we also coordinate with trusted workshops that create bespoke metal and mineral elements for us.

Stage 5

Installation in your home

Our team comes to your home and installs every element of the kitchen. We precisely adjust the mechanisms and perfect the finishing details, handing over a kitchen that is fully ready for everyday use.

Frequently asked questions

We work wherever exceptional interiors are being created. Our portfolio already includes more than 400 bespoke kitchens completed across Europe. We regularly visit cities such as Warsaw, Wrocław, Poznań, Kraków and Poland's Tricity, while our team can reach every corner of Poland. We also deliver international projects, creating wooden kitchens in Germany and the Scandinavian countries.

Our expertise lies in creating bespoke kitchens from solid wood. We often take a holistic approach to a project, however. If we are making your kitchen, we can also create wooden furniture for a pantry, wardrobe, bathroom or home library. This makes it possible to maintain a consistent character throughout the interior, although our work together always begins with the kitchen design.

Although “2026 kitchen trends” is one of the most frequently searched phrases, at Hul we believe an exceptional kitchen should rise above fashion. Trends are a wonderful source of inspiration and a way to discover what appeals to us, but when designing in solid wood, we look decades ahead. We want your interior to delight with its timelessness rather than reveal that it was created in one particular fashionable year. That is why we choose natural materials and enduring forms that mature alongside your home.

We buy our wood from local forest districts in Lower Silesia. We use only sustainably sourced timber, as confirmed by PEFC certification. This means forestry in our region is managed so that the amount of woodland continues to grow. Proceeds from timber sales allow foresters to care for new planting and nature conservation, preserving the forest's natural life cycle.
